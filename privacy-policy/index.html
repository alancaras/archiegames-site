Privacy Policy

This policy covers the apps published by Archie Games (The Trustee for ARC Trust): Aussie Rules Footy Coach and PairCall. The short version is that we collect as little as we possibly can, and we never sell or share anything.

Aussie Rules Footy Coach

Data collection. Aussie Rules Footy Coach does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information, except for the anonymous crash reporting described below. There are no accounts, logins or registration.

Local storage. All game data (saved games, team rosters, match results, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not accessible to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, all of it is permanently removed.

Crash reporting. If the app crashes, technical information about the crash (such as device model, operating system version, app version and what the app was doing at the time) is sent automatically to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can fix bugs. This data does not identify you personally and is not used for advertising.

Sponsor banners. The app periodically fetches a small list of in-game sponsor banners from a file we host. That request carries no information about you beyond what any web request includes. If you tap a banner, it opens the sponsor's website in your browser, where their own privacy policy applies.

No advertising or analytics. The app does not use advertising frameworks, tracking technologies or analytics beyond the crash reporting above. Other than Sentry, it does not integrate with any external service.

PairCall

What we store. PairCall stores only what it needs to ring your phones: your first name, your call schedule, the invite code that links you to your partner, and the device tokens needed to deliver calls and notifications. There are no accounts, passwords or feeds.

Calls. Calls are carried over an encrypted real-time connection. They are never recorded, and PairCall keeps no call history, transcripts or grading of any kind.

Crash reporting. As with Footy Coach, anonymous technical crash reports may be sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs.

Deleting your data. Leaving a pair or deleting the app removes your schedule and device registration from our server. If you would like us to confirm removal, use the contact form on our home page.

Children's privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data an app collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form on our home page.
